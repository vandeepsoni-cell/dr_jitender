About the Doctor

A neurologist's training, applied with attention to detail.

Dr. Jitender Singh Tuteja is a Consultant Neurologist based in Ahmedabad. He completed his M.D. at Smt. N.H.L. Municipal Medical College, Ahmedabad, followed by his D.N.B. in Neurology at Choithram Hospital and Research Centre.

He currently consults at EPIC Multispecialty Hospital in Bodakdev, where he is part of the hospital's Neurology & Interventional Neuroradiology department, and independently at his own practice, Dr. Jitendra Singh Clinic, in Satellite, Ahmedabad.

His published professional profile lists clinical focus in general and hospital-based neurology, including the evaluation and management of epilepsy, stroke, movement disorders and neurodegenerative conditions — outlined in more detail below.

Areas of Clinical Focus

Conditions commonly evaluated in neurology consultations.

The following reflects the clinical interest areas listed in Dr. Tuteja's published professional profiles. This is general educational information, not a diagnosis — an in-person consultation is required to assess any individual case.

01

Epilepsy & Seizure Disorders

Evaluation and long-term management of seizure disorders, including diagnostic work-up and treatment planning.

02

Stroke

Assessment of stroke symptoms and risk factors, with guidance on acute recognition and secondary prevention.

03

Parkinson's Disease & Movement Disorders

Consultation for tremor, rigidity and other movement disorder symptoms, and ongoing management of Parkinson's disease.

04

Alzheimer's Disease & Memory Disorders

Evaluation of memory loss and cognitive decline, and support for patients and families navigating a diagnosis.

05

ALS & Motor Neuron Disease

Diagnostic evaluation and coordinated long-term care planning for motor neuron disease.

06

Migraine & Headache Disorders

Assessment of recurrent or severe headache, including migraine, to identify triggers and treatment options.
